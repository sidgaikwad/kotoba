Glossary

The terminology this course uses consistently. Once a term is here, lessons use the Japanese term, not an English paraphrase.

This grows as we go. Terms are added when they are first taught, not when first mentioned in passing.

Register — the five categories

From 敬語の指針 T1 (文化審議会答申, 2007-02-02). This five-way split replaced the three-way 尊敬語 / 謙譲語 / 丁寧語 model that most textbooks still print.

TermReadingWhat it doesExample
敬語keigo The whole system of respectful language. Not a single "politeness level". —
尊敬語sonkeigo Raises the person you are talking about. Applied to their actions. いらっしゃる (they go/come/are)
謙譲語Ⅰkenjougo I Lowers your own action, toward the person that action touches. There must be a recipient who is being elevated. 伺う (I visit/ask someone respected)
謙譲語Ⅱ
= 丁重語		kenjougo II
teichougo		 Lowers your own action toward the listener, with no third party involved. This is the category the old three-way model could not express — the reason the government revised it. 参る (I go — said humbly to you)
丁寧語teineigo Politeness toward the listener, independent of who is being discussed. The です・ます layer. します, です
美化語bikago Beautification. Dresses up the word itself; respects nobody in particular. お茶, お酒

The distinction that matters most: 尊敬語 and 謙譲語 act on who is being talked about. 丁寧語 acts on who is being talked to. These are two independent dials and one verb can carry both at once. Collapsing them into a single "how polite am I being" scale is the root cause of most register errors by English speakers.

Social positioning

TermReadingMeaning
内uchi "Inside" — my group. Family, my team, my company. Membership is relative to the current conversation, not fixed.
外soto "Outside" — everyone else right now. Clients, other companies, strangers.
二重敬語nijuu-keigo Double honorifics — stacking two honorific mechanisms on one verb. Named in 敬語の指針 as an error, though extremely common in speech. おっしゃられる = おっしゃる + -られる.
空気を読むkuuki wo yomu "Read the air" — infer what was meant from what was not said. Concretely: hedges, hesitation, ちょっと…, and drawn-out inhales, all of which carry propositional content.

Grammar metalanguage

TermReadingMeaning
常体joutai Plain style — だ・である. Used in documents, specs, news, academic writing. Plain is not informal.
敬体keitai Polite style — です・ます. Used when addressing a listener.
自動詞jidoushi Intransitive verb — the thing acts on its own. サーバーが落ちる the server goes down.
他動詞tadoushi Transitive verb — someone acts on the thing. サーバーを落とす to take the server down. Choosing between the pair assigns responsibility.
連濁rendaku Sequential voicing — the second element of a compound voices its first consonant. 仕 + 事 koto → 仕事 shigoto.
